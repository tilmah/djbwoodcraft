Asonoha Table

Occasional table in Coachwood with glass top. Refined lightweight construction consisting of dovetail and through mortice and tenon joinery with a Kumiko top. Inspired by the Asanoha geometric pattern of Japanese craft.

Coachwood, Kumiko top, glass · 550 × 550 × 520 mm
